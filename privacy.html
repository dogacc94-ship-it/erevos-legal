Privacy Policy — Chimera

Chimera is a private tool used only by the owner of the Erevos Time YouTube channel.

It accesses YouTube data (channel analytics, video metadata, uploads) solely for that channel, using the owner's own Google account authorization.

All data is stored locally on the owner's machine. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
